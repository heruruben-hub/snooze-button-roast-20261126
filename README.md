An entry for the make.fun bounty "Snooze Button Roast": one line roasting an
alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```text
My alarm clock rings at 6 AM on a Saturday like it's gunning for Employee of the Month at a job that doesn't exist.
```

115 characters, one line.

## Why this line

The tired version of this joke is "I hate mornings" or "who invented the
alarm clock". This one gives the clock a fresh, specific job instead: an
over-eager employee clocking in for a shift nobody asked for, chasing a
promotion at a workplace that does not exist. The punchline lands in the
last few words — the clock is not just early, it is ambitious about
nothing — and the whole line is sayable in one breath, with a concrete
image (Employee of the Month) instead of vague grumbling.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
